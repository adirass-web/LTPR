## Excluded material

- Large MP4 masters in the public runtime or ordinary Git history
- Full third-party press PDFs or scans on the public site
- Cached social-media assets, duplicates, WhatsApp exports and weak low-resolution images
- Generic cybersecurity, military, flag or skyline imagery
- Unverified outlet claims or logo walls

Git history preserves the earlier handoff inventory and superseded readiness tables.
