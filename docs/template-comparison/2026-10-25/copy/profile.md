[profile-025] Applied work

[profile-026] Keeping power engineers in charge

[profile-027] Power engineers were responsible for keeping electricity flowing. A proposed cyber unit, staffed by outsiders, would change their operations while leaving them accountable. They resisted.

[profile-028] A new national cyber agency brought me in as a foreign expert. After studying local arrangements and interviewing sector professionals, I recommended keeping the engineers in charge, with cyber specialists alongside them.

[profile-029] Within weeks, the sector accepted my recommendations. Engineers helped design and review the changes, removing opposition and avoiding the cost of establishing and staffing a separate unit and duplicating systems.

[profile-030] New tools gave cyber specialists visibility across industrial control systems and IT: data they could correlate, use to establish normal behaviour, and monitor for anomalies—a foundation for measuring performance and deciding what to improve.
