## The editorial argument

**A person with a point of view → a record of application → the experience behind that perspective → its relevance to consequential decisions → an invitation without a sales pitch.**

These are editing stages, not five numbered sections on the website. Use only the headings needed for comprehension. Named work and sources do the credibility-building; never write that the subject is premium, discreet, enigmatic or internationally authoritative.

### 1. Introduce the person and the proposition together

Keep the name-only header. Beneath the quiet category “Strategic technology adviser,” compose the existing portrait directly with “Advantage is created, not bought.” The headline and portrait belong to the same opening unit; neither follows a separate biography or institutional credential.

First mobile composition to test: headline at logical start, portrait at logical end, with a full-width standfirst immediately beneath. The portrait retains its 2:3 ratio; no new crop or retouching. This is a deliberate alternative to A3's slogan, paragraph, then detached portrait/credential arrangement.

Retain the existing standfirst: “I work with governments and institutions to turn difficult technology and security problems into capabilities that work.” Move the laboratory role into the later provenance passage. A visitor should meet the person and the work before the academic affiliation.

### 2. Answer the proposition with work, not publicity

Lead with **World Bank / PROGRESS — Four critical sectors across 11 countries**. It is the stronger publicly documented application record. Proposed supporting copy: “Work across energy, healthcare, digital infrastructure and financial services translated sector-wide analysis into tailored recommendations.” A quiet source link identifies the 2025 field record. Keep that distinction explicit in review notes; confirm that the public-facing country label may be retained before publication. It does not block composition work.

Keep the approved disclosure once: “Only work already in the public record is named here.” No broadcast or newspaper image is placed as apparent evidence that either assignment succeeded. No invented client logos, report covers or project photographs.

Transition through an idea rather than a geographical chapter: **Technology is only part of the system.**

Proposed compressed copy: “Israel’s cyber advantage grew through connections between government, industry and universities. My work examines what can be transferred from that experience—and what cannot.” This is an adaptation of the existing approved copy, not a new biographical claim.

Bring the Springer book into this passage as a small published artifact, with title and co-author credit. Place the current laboratory role nearby as a quiet factual line. These establish Israeli provenance without a flag, skyline, nationality badge or an explanation of why Israel is impressive.

The NTV innovation-ecosystems still belongs here as a linked annotation, with a factual caption identifying an interview about innovation systems. It is evidence of public discussion of the subject, not evidence of operating the institution shown. Do not pair it with an unrelated press crop merely to create balance.

### 5. Close with the standard of work

End the argument with the existing principle: “The point is not another assessment. It is a capability that remains after the project ends.” Set it as a paragraph with modest emphasis through size and space, not a testimonial, quotation from a third party or large manifesto.

Use 8px for image-to-caption, 16px for paragraph relationships, 24px within an argument, and 40–48px between narrative movements. A source annotation stays closer to the sentence it supports than to the next topic. Do not assign the same large space above and below every picture.

Target the first work heading to begin within roughly the first 650px of the normal-size 390px composition; never enforce a fixed hero height or clip text to achieve it. The opener must establish the face and premise before the first scroll, without pushing evidence a full screen away.

At 320px, or when enlarged text no longer fits, the opening unit stacks in source order. At 390/430 the side-by-side opening is conditional on actual text fit, not a device assumption. No manual line-break exceptions. All article/broadcast annotations stay single-column on phones. On wider containers, an annotation may become a marginal figure beside the paragraph it belongs to; the page must not turn into a card grid.
